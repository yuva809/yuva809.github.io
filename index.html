I'm Yuvanesh, founder of Durby and an AI & Data Engineer based in Berlin. I recently completed my Master's in Data Science, and I build software end-to-end, from data pipelines and machine learning models to the products and interfaces people actually use.

Right now, I'm building Durby, an AI operations platform for restaurants. Alongside that, I lead AI & automation initiatives for The AI Collective's Berlin chapter. I also build my own AI products — DataPilot AI turns raw spreadsheets into dashboards and AI-generated insights in under a minute, while Trisearch AI is a hybrid search engine for enterprise documents, combining semantic search with keyword retrieval.

I previously led my university's Data Science Society for a year, running weekly workshops for 45+ students and growing membership by 30%. Good technical work and good communication aren't separate skills to me; a model or pipeline is only useful if someone else can understand it and act on it.
